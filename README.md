# AdClaw

**AdClaw** is an open-source AI operating layer for ad operations. It is designed to help teams plan, generate, check, launch, monitor, and optimize advertising campaigns with a multi-agent workflow.

The project focuses first on Meta Ads workflows, with a long-term goal of making AI-powered ad operations more accessible, transparent, and developer-friendly.

## What AdClaw Does

AdClaw helps automate the repetitive parts of running ads, including:

- Campaign strategy generation
- Competitor and audience research
- Ad copy and creative variant generation
- Compliance and risk checks before launch
- Campaign launch workflows
- Budget pacing and optimization suggestions
- Performance summaries and reporting
- AI assistant workflows for ad operations

## Why This Exists

Traditional ad management can be slow, expensive, and manual. Small businesses and developers often do not have access to full agency teams, growth analysts, or dedicated media buyers.

AdClaw explores how AI agents can reduce the workload required to operate campaigns while keeping the process transparent, inspectable, and open-source.

## Core Agent System

AdClaw is built around a multi-agent architecture:

### 1. Research Agent

Analyzes competitors, audiences, search intent, market positioning, and customer personas before campaign creation.

### 2. Creative Agent

Generates ad copy, hooks, creative directions, and multiple campaign variants for testing.

### 3. Launch & Optimization Agent

Assists with campaign setup, launch workflows, budget pacing, and optimization recommendations.

### 4. Reporting Agent

Summarizes campaign performance, highlights trends, and turns raw metrics into readable reports.

### 5. Compliance Agent

Checks ads against platform policies, brand guidelines, and risk rules before campaigns are launched.

### 6. AI Coach

Learns from previous campaign results and helps refine future strategies, tests, and recommendations.
